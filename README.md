## About Me

Hi! I'm **Glenn Gabriel Castillejo**, a Filipino cybersecurity enthusiast and fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (BSCpE) student at the **University of Perpetual Help System JONELTA – Cauayan Campus**, Isabela, Philippines.

I am passionate about cybersecurity, ethical hacking, digital forensics, reverse engineering, and solving complex technical challenges through Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions.

One of my biggest milestones was representing the Philippines as part of **Team Philippines at the ASEAN-Japan Cybersecurity Capacity Building Centre (AJCCBC) Cyber SEA Games 2026**, where our four-member team emerged as the overall champion in Bangkok, Thailand.

I believe cybersecurity is more than just finding vulnerabilities. It's about curiosity, continuous learning, critical thinking, and using technical skills to build a safer digital world.

> "Stay curious. Think like an attacker. Defend like a professional."
